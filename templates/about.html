A mini project

Lockart is a mini project built by a student team as part of a B.Tech course. It is a working prototype, not a real store: no real payments or deliveries happen, so please use fake details.

What makes it different: a Fair Visibility Ranking System (FVRS) that uses each shop's recent orders, rating, completion rate, distance and sales, gives new shops a fair start, and groups nearby orders into one delivery trip.

What we built

⚖️

Fair ranking (FVRS)

Shops with fewer recent orders earn more visibility, so busy shops do not always win.

🌱

New-shop boost

A new shop starts with full fairness points and a neutral rating, so it is not buried for being new.

🚚

Smart delivery grouping

A prototype that groups packed orders going to places within 1 km into one trip.

👥

Three roles

Customers shop, sellers manage products and orders, and the admin approves shops and handles complaints.

⭐

Reviews and complaints

Customers review shops and products after delivery, and raise complaints the admin can resolve.

🔒

Built with care

Hashed passwords, form protection against forged requests, and a lock after repeated wrong logins.
