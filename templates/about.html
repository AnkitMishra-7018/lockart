About Lockart

Lockart (Local Kart) is an online marketplace that brings small neighbourhood shops into e-commerce, and gives new and small sellers a fair chance to be seen.

A mini project

Lockart is a mini project built by a student team as part of a B.Tech course. It is a working prototype, not a real store: no real payments or deliveries happen, so please use fake details.

What makes it different: a Fair Visibility Ranking System (FVRS) that uses each shop's recent orders, rating, completion rate, distance and sales, gives new shops a fair start, and groups nearby orders into one delivery trip.
