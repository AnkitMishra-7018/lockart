Our story

Why we built Lockart

Big marketplaces mostly show the sellers who already get the most orders. Small neighbourhood shops can sell very good products, yet few customers ever find them.

Lockart ranks shops with FVRS, a fair visibility system: fewer recent orders earn more visibility, new shops start with a boost, and nearby orders can share one delivery trip.
